## What to expect

Every tier supports the same open-source work. Sponsorship is optional, and the projects keep their existing licenses. It doesn't buy a guaranteed response time, a particular feature, or a support contract. You can change or cancel your sponsorship through GitHub whenever you need to.

If money is tight, please keep it. Useful bug reports, a small fix, clearer documentation, or telling someone about a project are all appreciated too. Thanks for being here.
